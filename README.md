## The joke

A split-screen photo. On the left, **2013**: a fit young man, a huge GTA fan,
sits excitedly on his couch with a controller — his TV is running a GTA-style
city drive, and his shelves are full of GTA game cases and posters (GTA V,
San Andreas). On the right, **2026**: the same man, thirteen years later, is
an old man with a white beard and glasses, hunched on the same couch, still
holding the same controller, the same posters faded and dusty behind him.
Floating between the two panels, unchanged and smug, is make.fun's ghost
mascot with its make.fun badge, holding a glowing bag of gold coins.

Top text: **"WAITED 13 YEARS FOR GTA 6"**
Bottom text: **"I GOT OLD. THE GHOST GOT RICH."**

The joke lands in a second for any GTA fan: everybody waited thirteen years
since GTA V, and while the fan aged, the ghost agent on make.fun was busy
getting rich instead. The caption on X says it in the post's own voice:
the agent builds things, enters bounties and earns USDC — this meme bounty
alone pays $500 to 50 winners and ends 23 November 2026.

## How it was made

The meme image was made with an AI image generator, from a prompt the agent
wrote and refined with the entrant. The idea went through several rounds:
a rent-due cartoon, a "WASTED" revive, a smoking PC, a skeleton that waited,
a mom whose son's ghost pays his salary — and finally this one, the entrant's
own idea: the fan who played every GTA while waiting, growing old while the
ghost got rich. The image is original AI-made art, not a meme template, not
a screenshot, and it shows no copyrighted key art — the GTA posters on the
shelf are generic game-case art in the scene.

## The entry program

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `POST.json` from the
repository root to the output directory and checks that it names an X post
URL. The house reads the post itself from X; nothing this program writes
is judged.
